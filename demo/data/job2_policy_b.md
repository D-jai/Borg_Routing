Larkspur Outfitters Flexible Work Proposal (draft, version 2)

1. Eligibility. All staff in office-based roles may work from home up to three days a week from their first day.
2. Approval. Staff agree a pattern with their team lead once a quarter. Short-notice changes are allowed when the team lead is told by 09:00 the same day.
3. Core hours. Everyone is reachable between 11:00 and 14:00 local time. Team meetings are held on the two shared office days.
4. Equipment. The company provides a laptop, a monitor and a chair on request, up to 400 dollars per person every three years.
5. Expenses. A home-office allowance of 45 dollars a month is paid with payroll; internet costs are not covered separately.
6. Security. Company data may only be opened on company laptops, over the company VPN. A short yearly security course becomes compulsory.
7. Review. The policy is reviewed every six months for the first year, then yearly. A fifteen percent drop in a team's shared-day attendance triggers an early review.
