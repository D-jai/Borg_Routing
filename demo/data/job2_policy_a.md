# DEMO DATA for recorded demo runs. Fictional; cited by no claim. Job 2, document A: current policy of a fictional company (Larkspur Outfitters).

Larkspur Outfitters Remote Work Policy (current, version 1)

1. Eligibility. Staff in office-based roles may work from home up to two days a week once they have finished their first three months.
2. Approval. The team lead approves a fixed weekly pattern. Changes need two working days of notice.
3. Core hours. Everyone is reachable between 10:00 and 15:00 local time on every working day.
4. Equipment. The company provides a laptop and a monitor. Other equipment is paid by the employee.
5. Expenses. A flat home-office allowance of 20 dollars a month is paid with payroll.
6. Security. Company data may only be opened on company laptops, over the company VPN.
7. Review. The policy is reviewed once a year by the people team.
