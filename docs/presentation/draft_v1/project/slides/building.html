Say the limit out loud: facts a local step extracts, such as a label or a name, can travel onward inside its answer. The amount is capped, long copied passages are blocked and the run record shows every request that left. Anyone who cannot accept that uses the all-local setting. The operator’s own typed request goes to the planner, so documents are handed over as a folder, never pasted in. The small line: BORG_ROUTE measures each local model’s speed in tokens a second (C90) and counts the waiting time in the cost it compares (C91). What was measured on a laptop, from round 2 (bench/results/2026_11_18/summary.md): on the development laptop’s CPU the local model answered 12 of 32 item calls in about 67 minutes and was then stopped. No figure was measured on a machine with a GPU. The private lane is a setting you turn on; round 3 of the benchmark ran with it off.
